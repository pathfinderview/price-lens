# Launch copy — draft, not posted

I made a tiny travel app: point your camera at a price tag and see what it costs in rupees.

Choose a photo, tap the detected price, and convert. Photos stay on your device. No signup.

Try Price Lens: https://pathfinderview.github.io/price-lens/

First beta. I'd love to know which real-world labels confuse it.

## 20-second demo shot list
1. Open the app on a phone, source THB and home INR.
2. Scan a clearly printed sample price label.
3. Tap the detected 589.00 chip; show the rupee result and dated rate.
4. Change the home currency; show the new conversion.
5. End on URL. Do not imply continuous live overlays or a native App Store release.
