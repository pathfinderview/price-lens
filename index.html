A SMALL TRAVEL COMPANION

Prices, translated.

Choose the currency on the price tag and your home currency, then open the live camera. Hold a clear price in view. Converted amounts appear over the detected numbers automatically—no photo or tap needed.

A few honest details

Recognition runs repeatedly on your live camera feed, not on every video frame. A price must be read twice before appearing. Speed depends on your phone; it may take a few seconds. Old labels disappear when no longer detected.

OCR can misread labels or mistake product codes, dates and weights for prices. Confirm the original number and selected currency. Daily Frankfurter reference rates exclude bank fees; the date and any saved-rate fallback are shown below.

Privacy

Camera frames are processed on your device and are not uploaded, saved as photos or recorded. Currency choices and rates are saved in this browser. No accounts, ads or analytics. The host, font and rate providers receive ordinary web requests, not camera images.

Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost and your permission. Closing the camera or leaving the app stops the camera. Add to Home Screen is available where your browser supports it. First use downloads the on-device recognition model.
