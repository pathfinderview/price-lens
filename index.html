A couple of honest details

Reading a price may take a few seconds. Check the number: weights, dates and product codes can be mistaken for prices. The first use downloads the on-device scanner.

Reference rates exclude bank fees. Saved rates are clearly marked. If you leave the app, your camera stops.

Your camera stays yours.

Frames are processed on this device, never uploaded or recorded. Currency choices and rates are saved in your browser. Hosting, font and rate services receive ordinary web requests—not your images. No analytics or ads.
