Prices, translated.

Choose the currency on the price tag and your home currency. Open the rear camera, take a still scan, or choose a photo. Tap a detected number to convert it. You can always edit it manually.

A few honest details

This beta scans still frames, not continuous AR overlays. OCR can misread labels: confirm the detected number and selected currency. For best results, use a sharp, well-lit photo of one price label.

Daily reference rates come from Frankfurter’s public API. They are indicative, not your bank’s settlement rate, and exclude card fees and markups. The app shows the rate date and marks saved rates.

Privacy

Images are processed locally with Tesseract.js and are not uploaded. Your currency choices and exchange-rate cache are saved in this browser. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics. Your host and rate provider receive ordinary web requests; the rate request includes the selected currency pair, not your photo or price.

Camera access needs HTTPS or localhost. You can install this web app using your browser’s Add to Home Screen option where supported. First-time scanning downloads the recognition model.
